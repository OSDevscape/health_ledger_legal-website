1. Privacy overview

Health Ledger is a free Android application provided by OSDevscape. It works without user accounts or cloud synchronization. Health records entered into the app are stored locally on your device and are not automatically sent to OSDevscape.

Health Ledger does not include advertising, analytics services, third-party crash-reporting services, or tracking tools. OSDevscape does not sell your health records or use them for advertising.

2. Information stored in the app

Depending on how you use the app, Health Ledger may store:

  • Medication names, dosages, forms, instructions, quantities, and notes.
  • Medication schedules, reminder times, and start or end dates.
  • Dose statuses and recorded completion times.
  • Glucose readings, units, dates, times, meal context, and notes.
  • App preferences, including appearance and display settings.

This information is used locally to provide schedules, reminders, dose tracking, and glucose history. You choose what information to enter.

3. Information OSDevscape receives

OSDevscape does not receive your health records through normal use of the app.

If you email OSDevscape, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to include. Support correspondence is used to respond to requests, resolve issues, and maintain necessary support records. It is retained only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes or legal obligations.

Email is handled through our email service provider and is subject to that provider’s processing practices. Please do not send sensitive records or backup files unless necessary for your request.

4. Permissions and notifications

Health Ledger may request notification permission and, on supported Android versions, access to schedule alarms close to your selected times. Local storage and file-handling features support records and manual backup export and import. These functions do not upload health records to OSDevscape.

You can manage permissions in Android settings. Disabling them may prevent related features from working. Reminder notifications may reveal medication information on your lock screen; adjust notification visibility through device settings.

5. Manual backups and sharing

Health Ledger provides manual backup export and import. Exported backups may contain sensitive medication and glucose information. Keep them in a location you trust.

If you share or upload a backup through another application or service, that information becomes subject to the recipient’s or service’s practices. OSDevscape does not receive a copy merely because you export it.

The app does not provide its own automatic cloud backup or synchronization service.

6. Retention and deletion

Records remain on your device until you delete them, clear app data, or otherwise remove the stored information. You can delete individual records where supported or use Clear all data.

Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling the app generally removes its private local records. Exported backups must be deleted separately. Copies shared with others are not removed when you delete records from Health Ledger.

OSDevscape cannot access, retrieve, or remotely delete local records it does not possess.

7. Security

No device or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your device with a screen lock, keep software updated, and use care when exporting or sharing backups.

Do not assume exported backup files are encrypted. Anyone who can access a readable backup may be able to view its contents.

8. Children and families

Health Ledger is available to users of all ages, including children using it with a parent, guardian, or responsible caregiver. It does not require children to create accounts or submit personal information to OSDevscape. App records are handled locally as described above.

Parents and guardians should supervise children’s use and avoid sending children’s sensitive information through support email. Contact OSDevscape if you believe a child has sent personal information and want it deleted.

9. Distribution and other services

Health Ledger is distributed through Google Play and direct APK downloads. Google Play, the website hosting an APK, email providers, and file-sharing services may handle information under their own privacy policies.

This policy describes Health Ledger and OSDevscape’s handling of information; it does not replace those services’ policies.

10. Changes to this policy

OSDevscape may update this policy when the app or its information handling changes. Updated policies will show a new effective date. Material changes will be communicated through the app or an accompanying update notice. Consent will be requested where required by law.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests about information you have sent to us, contact OSDevscape at OSDevscape@gmail.com.
